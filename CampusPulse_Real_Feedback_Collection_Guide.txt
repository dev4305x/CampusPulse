CAMPUSPULSE — REAL FEEDBACK COLLECTION & LABELING GUIDE
===========================================================

Purpose
-------
Collect anonymized campus feedback and label it for supervised ML training
and evaluation.

IMPORTANT
---------
Do not collect names, phone numbers, register numbers, email addresses,
personal addresses, medical information, or other unnecessary identifiers.
Participation should be voluntary and the feedback should be anonymized.

CSV columns
-----------
feedback_id : unique ID
feedback    : exact anonymized feedback text
sentiment   : positive / neutral / negative
category    : one of the categories below
location    : optional broad campus location
date        : YYYY-MM-DD
notes       : optional labeling note

Allowed categories
------------------
Wi-Fi
Food
Hostel
Transport
Academics
Laboratory
Library
Infrastructure
Cleanliness
Administration
Safety
Events

Sentiment labeling
------------------
positive = mainly praises or reports a satisfactory experience
neutral  = factual/mixed/ambiguous without a clearly positive or negative tone
negative = mainly reports a problem, dissatisfaction, failure, delay, or poor experience

Examples
--------
"The WiFi works perfectly in the library."
positive | Wi-Fi

"The bus usually comes around 8 AM."
neutral | Transport

"The evening bus is always overcrowded."
negative | Transport

"The mess food is okay, but there isn't much variety."
neutral | Food

"The projector is broken again."
negative | Laboratory

Recommended collection target
-----------------------------
Start with at least 300–500 real, anonymized feedback records.
For a stronger model, aim for 1,000+ records.

Quality rules
-------------
1. Preserve the student's original wording where possible.
2. Do not rewrite feedback to make the label obvious.
3. Include short, long, casual, and grammatically imperfect feedback.
4. Keep mixed/ambiguous feedback instead of forcing a negative label.
5. Review labels consistently before training.
6. Keep a separate untouched test set for final evaluation.

Suggested split
---------------
70% training
15% validation
15% final test

The final test set must not be used while tuning the model.
