CampusPulse V6 — Quality & Robustness Update

Problem identified from live dashboard test
-------------------------------------------
Input: "Im really tired of all this exams and test"
V5 output: Positive (71%), Academics (42%)
The category was directionally correct, but the sentiment was a clear conversational-language error.

Fixes in V6
------------
1. Added a context-aware student-language layer for common dissatisfaction phrases:
   tired of, fed up, frustrated, exhausted, stressed, too many, too frequent,
   terrible, not satisfied, no seats, super slow, etc.
2. Added domain cues for unambiguous categories such as Wi-Fi, Library, Laboratory,
   Transport, Food, Hostel and examinations.
3. Low-confidence predictions are explicitly flagged as "Needs review" instead of
   being presented as certain.
4. Related-complaint retrieval now combines word and character TF-IDF, which helps
   with casual/spelling variants and short complaints.
5. Related-complaint results are restricted to the predicted broad category and
   duplicate complaint text is collapsed.
6. Live submissions are included in related-complaint retrieval.
7. Dashboard now shows confidence/review status and the fact that context-aware
   language checks were applied.

Important limitation
--------------------
These are targeted robustness checks, not a claim of real-world accuracy. The
statistical models are still trained primarily on public student-feedback data
and synthetic CampusPulse data. Human review remains appropriate for low-confidence
or ambiguous feedback.
